# Sharp hypercontractivity for free group von Neumann algebras

This project uses Lean to verify the Fourier-polynomial form of [Theorem 1.1](#theorem-1-1) and the group-algebra inequality in [Theorem 1.2](#theorem-1-2).

> Xinyuan Xie and Haonan Zhang, *Sharp hypercontractivity for free group von Neumann algebras*, [arXiv:2606.03423v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03423v3).

## Mathematical statements

Let $\mathbb{F}_n$ be the free group on $n\geq 2$ generators, and let $|g|$ denote reduced word length. Write $\widehat{\mathbb{F}_n}$ for the group von Neumann algebra generated by the left regular representation $\lambda$ on $\ell_2(\mathbb{F}_n)$. Its canonical tracial state satisfies $\tau(\lambda_g)=\delta_{g,e}$. The Poisson semigroup $P_t=e^{-tL}$ and its generator satisfy

$$
P_t(\lambda_g)=e^{-t|g|}\lambda_g,\qquad L(\lambda_g)=|g|\lambda_g.
$$

The tracial norm is $\|x\|_p=\tau(|x|^p)^{1/p}$. For positive $a$, write $\operatorname{Ent}(a)=\tau(a\log a)-\tau(a)\log\tau(a)$, with $0\log 0=0$.

<a name="theorem-1-1"></a>

**Theorem 1.1.** Let $1<p\leq q<\infty$ and $t\geq 0$. Then

$$
\|P_t x\|_q\leq\|x\|_p,\qquad x\in\mathbb{C}[\mathbb{F}_n],
$$

holds if and only if

$$
t\geq\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{q-1}{p-1}.
$$

<a name="theorem-1-2"></a>

**Theorem 1.2.** For every positive $x\in\mathbb{C}[\mathbb{F}_n]$,

$$
\operatorname{Ent}(x^2)\leq 2\tau(xLx).
$$

The Lean statements above are on $\mathbb{C}[\mathbb{F}_n]$. The paper states Theorem 1.1 on $L_p(\widehat{\mathbb{F}_n})$ and extends the inequality of Theorem 1.2 to the form domain of $L^{1/2}$.
