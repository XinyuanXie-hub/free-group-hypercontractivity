# Sharp hypercontractivity for free group von Neumann algebras

This project uses Lean to verify [Theorem 1.1](#theorem-1-1) and [Theorem 1.2](#theorem-1-2).

> Xinyuan Xie and Haonan Zhang, *Sharp hypercontractivity for free group von Neumann algebras*, [arXiv:2606.03423v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03423v3).

## Mathematical statements

Let $`\mathbb{F}_n`$ be the free group on $`n\geq 2`$ generators and $`|g|`$ the reduced word length of $`g`$. Write $`\widehat{\mathbb{F}_n}`$ for the group von Neumann algebra generated by the left regular representation $`\lambda`$, with canonical tracial state $`\tau(\lambda_g)=\delta_{g,e}`$. The associated noncommutative $`L_p`$ norm is $`\lVert x\rVert_p=\tau(|x|^p)^{1/p}`$.

The Poisson semigroup $`(P_t)_{t\geq 0}`$ satisfies

$$
P_t(\lambda_g)=e^{-t|g|}\lambda_g,\qquad L(\lambda_g)=|g|\lambda_g.
$$

<a name="theorem-1-1"></a>

**Theorem 1.1.** Let $`1\lt p\leq q\lt \infty`$. Then

$$
\lVert P_t x\rVert_q\leq\lVert x\rVert_p,\qquad x\in L_p(\widehat{\mathbb{F}_n}),
$$

holds if and only if

$$
t\geq\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{q-1}{p-1}.
$$

For positive $`a`$, put $`\mathrm{Ent}(a)=\tau(a\log a)-\tau(a)\log\tau(a)`$.

<a name="theorem-1-2"></a>

**Theorem 1.2.** For every positive $`x\in\mathbb{C}[\mathbb{F}_n]`$,

$$
\mathrm{Ent}(x^2)\leq 2\tau(xLx).
\tag{1.1}
$$

The inequality extends by closure to the natural form domain of $`L^{1/2}`$.
